Tab previews stay on this device

QuickTab remembers recent tab titles, URLs, and icons and automatically takes screenshots of visible web tabs as you browse. This data is used only to show recent tabs and their thumbnails in the switcher.

The list and thumbnails stay in Chrome’s session storage on this device and clear when you quit Chrome. Nothing is sent or shared. Privacy policy
