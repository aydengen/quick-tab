QuickTab

Panel stays open without holding Alt. Use j/k or arrows to navigate, Enter to switch.

Maximum recent tabs in the switcher, including the current tab.

Include chrome://, Web Store and other restricted pages in the tab list.

Tab previews stay on this device

When enabled, QuickTab remembers recent tab titles, URLs, and icons and automatically captures visible web tabs for switcher thumbnails. The list and thumbnails stay in Chrome’s session storage and clear when you quit Chrome. Nothing is sent or shared.

To allow local previews, open the quick start guide.
